Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers Kaya Kurrent's pages and "Kurrent Pages" (Meta app ID 1665059161685856), the app we use to publish to them.

What the app does

Kurrent Pages is used only by the Kaya Kurrent team. It publishes Kaya Kurrent's own posts to Kaya Kurrent's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and Threads account, and reads back each post's ID, link and time so we know what went out.

Information we collect

Only the pages and accounts we manage (and the access tokens Meta issues for them), the content we publish ourselves, and the IDs, links and times of those posts. We do not collect or store personal information about people who visit, follow, like, comment on or share our posts; those interactions are covered by each platform's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and share nothing with third parties except Meta and X as needed to publish our posts.

Storage

Post records are kept in our private working files. Access tokens are stored as protected secrets.

Children

Our pages are news services for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

Any update will appear here with a new date. Questions: kayakurrent@gmail.com

Data deletion

The Kurrent Pages app holds no personal data about members of the public.

Remove the app from your Facebook account

Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → find "Kurrent Pages" → Remove.

Ask us to delete your data

Email kayakurrent@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the account the request is about. We will confirm and delete any information we hold about you within 30 days.
